Delete Your MedSched Account

App: MedSched  |  Developer: Gean  |  Last updated: October 24, 2026

You can ask us to delete your MedSched account and the data linked to it at any time. Follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Open your email app and sign in to the same email address you used to register in MedSched.
  2. Send a message to [tonksgaius@gmail.com] with the subject "MedSched account deletion request".
  3. In the message, include the name you registered with and write: "Please delete my MedSched account and its data."
  4. We will confirm by reply and delete your account within 30 days of receiving the request.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Account record in Firebase Authentication: name, email address, user ID, and passwordPermanently deleted.
Any Doctor AI questions you sentMedSched does not keep a copy on its own servers. Handling by the AI provider is described in our Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your phone

Your medications, schedules, and dose history are stored only on your device and are not held by us. To remove them, open Android Settings → Apps → MedSched → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This cannot be undone.

What is kept

We do not keep any personal data after your account is deleted, apart from records we are legally required to retain, if any. Firebase may keep routine backup copies for a short period before they are permanently erased.

Delete some data without deleting your account

Inside the app, you can delete individual medicines and schedules from My Medications and Daily Schedule. You can also change your display name from Profile.

Contact

Questions about deletion? Email [tonksgaius@gmail.com].
