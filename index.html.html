MedSched Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026  |  Last updated: October 23, 2026

MedSched ("the app") is a medication reminder and adherence tracking application for Android, developed by Breneth Gean B. Lican as a student project at Ifugao State University, College of Computing Sciences, Lamut, Ifugao, Philippines. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhere it is storedWhy it is used
Account details: name, email address, password, and a user IDFirebase Authentication (Google cloud). Passwords are handled by Firebase and are never visible to the developer.To create your account, sign you in and out, and send password-reset emails.

MedSched does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, or payment information.

2. How we use information

  • To let you create an account and sign in before using the app.
  • To schedule dose reminders and refill alerts on your device.
  • To record and summarize your adherence history.
  • To answer your questions in Doctor AI.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing.

3. Data stored on your device

Your medications, schedules, and dose logs stay on your phone. They are not uploaded to any server by MedSched and are not linked to your Firebase account in the cloud. Because of this, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data. Your Firebase account is stored in the cloud and remains until you ask for it to be deleted.

4. Third-party services

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): processes your name, email, password, and user ID to run sign-in. See Firebase privacy information.
  • AI chat service (Doctor AI): when you send a question, the text is transmitted to a cloud AI provider to generate a reply. Do not enter information that identifies you or other people in your questions. [Provider name and link to its privacy policy to be added once the service is finalized.]

No data is shared with third parties beyond what these services need to work.

5. Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications: to show dose reminders and refill alerts.
  • Exact alarms: so reminders arrive at the scheduled minute.
  • Run at device startup: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Internet access: for sign-in, password reset, and Doctor AI. Reminders and medication data work without internet once you are signed in.

6. Medical disclaimer

MedSched is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Doctor AI gives general information only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always ask your doctor or pharmacist about your medicines. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

7. Data security

Account sign-in is protected by Firebase's security systems and encrypted connections. Locally stored data relies on your device's own protections, so we recommend using a screen lock. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Local data: you can delete individual medications and schedules in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Account data: to delete your Firebase account and the name and email stored with it, email the address in section 11 from your registered email. We will delete it within 30 days.

9. Children's privacy

MedSched is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date above will show the latest revision, and significant changes will be noted in the app or on this page.
